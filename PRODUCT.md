# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Young professionals managing 2-5 credit cards with inconsistent spending patterns. Subscription-heavy users paying for 10+ recurring services who lose track of costs. Predictive planners who want to know their financial trajectory 30-60 days out. Debt optimizers actively working to eliminate credit card debt with a smart strategy.

Context: they open this app to understand what is about to happen to their money, not what already happened. Primary task on any given screen is either a risk check ("am I about to overdraft?"), a decision ("can I afford this?"), or a strategy session ("how do I pay this off faster?").

## Product Purpose

ArgusAI is an AI-powered financial intelligence system. Not a budgeting app. It predicts financial risk, reasons about spending behavior, and helps users make smarter money decisions before problems occur.

Existing finance apps (Mint, YNAB, Copilot, Monarch) are backward-looking — they show what you already spent. ArgusAI is forward-looking: it simulates, forecasts, and reasons about what is about to happen to your finances, and what to do about it now.

Success looks like: a user opens the app, immediately understands their financial risk level for the next 30 days, and leaves with a concrete action they would not have taken otherwise.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, Watchful, Premium.

Voice: confident and precise, never alarmist. Speaks like a financial analyst who has already done the work and is telling you the conclusion. No hedging, no generic disclaimers embedded in UI copy. When there is risk, it names it specifically.

Tone: calm authority. The interface should feel like it already knows something important and is about to tell you.

## Anti-references

- **Mint / YNAB**: soft pastels, budget-tracker aesthetic, consumer-friendly but shallow. ArgusAI is not a color-by-category spending app.
- **Bloomberg Terminal**: dense data overload, no hierarchy, intimidating wall of numbers. ArgusAI surfaces intelligence, not raw data.
- **Notion / Linear (neutral SaaS gray)**: personality-free, looks like every other productivity SaaS. ArgusAI has a distinct identity anchored to its copper+red brand.

## Design Principles

1. **Forward over backward.** Every screen surfaces what is coming, not what passed. Forecasts, risk scores, and predictions lead; transaction history supports.
2. **Intelligence earns trust.** Every AI output is grounded in real account data with traceable reasoning. No generated figures, no vague advice. Specificity is the proof.
3. **Severity speaks clearly.** Risk levels, alerts, and health scores communicate urgency without panic. A user should know within 2 seconds whether their financial state requires action today.
4. **Premium feels effortless.** Complexity is hidden behind an elegant, composure-first interface. Dense data is fine; cluttered presentation is not.
5. **Guardian, not gatekeeper.** ArgusAI watches, warns, and suggests. It never blocks or lectures. The user remains in control; the AI is the analyst in their corner.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. All text contrast ratios at 4.5:1 or above. Reduced motion support required on all animations. Color is never the sole conveyor of meaning — severity states use icon + color + label together. Financial figures use tabular numerals for alignment.
